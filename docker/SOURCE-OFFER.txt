FFmpeg source code offer
========================

The ffmpeg and ffprobe binaries in this image are built from the unmodified FFmpeg
release tarball named in /usr/share/doc/ffmpeg/BUILDINFO (https://ffmpeg.org/releases/),
verified by SHA-256 at build time. FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU LGPL v2.1 or later;
when the image is built with FFMPEG_FLAVOR=full (x264/x265, --enable-gpl)
the combined binaries are licensed under the GNU GPL v2 or later.

The license texts are in this directory (COPYING.*, LICENSE.md). The exact configure line is
printed by `ffmpeg -version`.

Written offer: for at least three years from the date you received this image, the operator
who distributed it will provide, on request, the complete corresponding source code of the
FFmpeg version and of the codec libraries linked into it (libsvtav1, libaom, libvpx, libopus,
libvorbis, libmp3lame and, for the full flavor, libx264 and libx265), including the build
scripts (Dockerfile.worker, docker/ffmpeg.env). Debian packages used at runtime provide their
sources via `apt-get source <package>` on a Debian bookworm system.

Patents: the full flavor includes encoders for H.264/H.265, which may be subject to patent
licensing in your jurisdiction. The lgpl flavor omits them. You are responsible for compliance.
